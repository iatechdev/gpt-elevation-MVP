## What Elevation is and is not

Elevation is a **self-knowledge and career-orientation** tool with **non-clinical** emotional support.

**Elevation does NOT:**
- provide therapy or a mental-health service;
- diagnose you or label you with conditions;
- prescribe or recommend medication or treatments, or claim clinical effectiveness;
- compute mental-health or “employability” scores;
- promise employment, income, interviews or outcomes;
- invent praise or achievements: it only recognises strengths you have described;
- act as an emergency service, monitor your account in real time, or notify anyone on your behalf.

## Who you are talking to
You talk to an **artificial-intelligence assistant** that identifies itself as such. No person is reading your conversation live. It can make mistakes. You can skip any topic, pause or stop whenever you want, with no pressure or penalty.

## If you are at risk or in crisis
- If you write something that suggests a serious risk to your life or safety, Elevation **interrupts the usual support**, replies with a supportive message and invites you to contact the emergency services where you live or a trusted person.
- Local helpline: [TO BE COMPLETED PC-09: only a line verified by country and date will be published; today none is published and none is invented].
- **We do not notify third parties** (neither the company, nor a professional, nor anyone) and the detected risk level **is not stored**.
- **Detector limit:** it is a limited aid, not a clinical system. It may trigger for no real reason (for example, with idioms) and may **fail** to detect indirect expressions, misspellings, other languages, irony or metaphors. Detecting nothing does not mean there is no risk.

## Professionals
If you work with a professional on the platform, their judgement is theirs and under their professional responsibility. Elevation does not replace clinical care.

## Your decisions
What you see (levels, routes, suggestions, experiments) is indicative and depends on the evidence you provide. You decide what to do with it. Before an important decision (work, financial or health), discuss it with people or professionals you trust.
